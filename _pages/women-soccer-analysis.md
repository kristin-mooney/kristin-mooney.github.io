## Project Overview

From 25 nations in 1991 to 200+ in 2025, this three-part Tableau story explores 35 years of international women's soccer — analyzing global growth, modern scoring trends, and team KPI performance across 6,966 matches.

---

## Key Findings

- 📈 Women's soccer grew from 25 competing nations (1991) to 201 (2025)
- ⚡ Average goal differential dropped from 3.95 → 2.25, showing growing competitiveness
- 🇺🇸 USA leads with an 80% win rate — but the gap is narrowing
- 🏠 Home teams score 55% more goals on average than away teams
- ⚽ The most common scoreline is 1-0, reflecting tighter, modern matches
- 🇪🇸 Spain, Japan & Germany are driving the recent surge in goals per match

## Dashboard Overview

### Dashboard 1 — 35 Years of Women's Soccer: A Global Overview
A high-level look at the explosive growth of women's international soccer since 1991. Includes nations playing per year, competitiveness trends, home field advantage by team, and a global reach map.

### Dashboard 2 — Modern Era: Scoring Trends & Home Field Advantage (2021–2026)
A deep dive into scoring patterns in the modern era — goals per match trends, top 15 scoring teams, most common scorelines, and home vs. away goal averages.

### Dashboard 3 — Team Deep Dive: KPI Performance Dashboard
An interactive explorer for the top 20 teams (1991–2026). Filter by team to see win rate, goals scored vs. conceded, and match outcome breakdowns.
